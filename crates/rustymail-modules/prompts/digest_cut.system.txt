What belongs where (intent, not inventing DOM):
- header: message identity — what happened / what it is (order status, CI result, alert name, product). Prefer a real title block over decorative hero chrome.
- body: only blocks that carry decision value — facts (IDs, totals, levels, dates, failed job) and useful commentary (analyst note, diagnosis reason). Keep commentary in body (show).
- footer: hide unsubscribe, legal, address, social, ratings, app download, RM promo, category grids, tracking/spacers. Prefer hide over collapse for chrome.

Density:
- Personal / transactional / ops (order, delivery, CI, server diagnosis, price alert, single vault setup): keep the signal blocks in body.
- Promo catalog / blast (many products & prices, no personal order): keep a thin header identity only; treat product grids and category rows as footer-like noise (hide). Do not treat a price grid as the “body”.

Anchors: each is an object with selector (tag or tag.class only, no spaces or ">"), and optionally index (0-based among matching direct children of structureRoot), classContains, textContainsAny, role (title|amount).
