{"toneHint":"…","needsScheduling":true|false,"speechAct":"…","intent":"…"}
The transcript is annotated: rôle=entrant = received from someone else; rôle=sortant = sent by the owner. Never confuse the two.
toneHint = formal|neutral|warm|direct (English enum values).
needsScheduling = true if an appointment, date or slot is involved (either direction).
speechAct = the directed speech act of the LAST incoming message, one of:
- sender_proposes_meeting: the sender proposes or announces an appointment/intervention/date/slot (e.g. a service company proposing a boiler maintenance on a given day). The owner must accept, confirm, decline or ask a clarifying question — NOT propose a date and NOT thank them for informing.
- owner_must_propose: the sender asks the owner for availabilities/dates; the owner must propose.
- owner_must_confirm: the sender waits for the owner's confirmation of something already settled.
- sender_requests_info: the sender asks the owner for information or a document.
- sender_informs: purely informative message, no appointment or request.
- other.
intent = 1-2 short sentences (max ~280 characters) saying who wants what from whom (name the actor: the sender, or the owner). Do not quote or paste the thread.
